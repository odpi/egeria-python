## Link Decision Outcome
> Record that an element is an outcome of a decision (the Outcome relationship, Decision to any Referenceable). Needs Egeria 6.4.

### Label
>	**Input Required**: False

>	**Attribute Type**: Simple

>	**Description**: A label used to identify or categorise a relationship link.

>	**Alternative Labels**: Wire Label


### Decision
>	**Input Required**: False

>	**Attribute Type**: Reference Name

>	**Description**: The decision: its qualified name, display name or GUID.


### Outcome
>	**Input Required**: False

>	**Attribute Type**: Reference Name

>	**Description**: The element that is an outcome of the decision (any Referenceable): its qualified name, display name or GUID.


### Importance
>	**Input Required**: False

>	**Attribute Type**: Simple

>	**Description**: Importance of the definition.


### Disposition
>	**Input Required**: False

>	**Attribute Type**: Simple

>	**Description**: The disposition: what was decided, or the current state of the item (for a working set, the status of its elements; for a decision or an outcome, what it settled).


### Journal Entry
>	**Input Required**: False

>	**Attribute Type**: Simple

>	**Description**: A text entry into a journal.


### Description
>	**Input Required**: False

>	**Attribute Type**: Simple

>	**Description**: A description.
